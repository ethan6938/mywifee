Open Your Letter

Dear Jess ❤️

I wanted to make you something that was completely and genuinely for you. Not because a website can magically make someone fall in love, but because sometimes words don't feel like enough when there is so much you want someone to know. I wanted you to have a little place you could come back to whenever you needed a reminder of how special you are to me.

Jess, there is something about you that is difficult to put into a few simple words. You have become someone I think about in the little moments — when something makes me smile, when I hear a song that reminds me of you, or when I simply wish I could tell you something and hear your reaction. Somehow, you have a way of making ordinary moments feel a little more meaningful just by being part of them.

I love the little things about you just as much as the big things. Your smile, your personality, the way you talk, the way you care, and all those tiny things you probably don't even realise people notice. You don't need to be perfect for me to appreciate you. You don't need to change yourself or become someone else. The person you are is already someone worth appreciating.

If I could give you anything, I would want it to be the ability to see yourself the way someone who truly cares about you sees you. I would want you to see the good in yourself on the days when you struggle to find it. I would want you to know that your presence matters, that your smile matters, and that the little pieces of yourself you sometimes overlook can mean so much to someone else.

I can't promise that every day will be perfect, and I don't want to pretend that feelings can be forced or written into existence. What I can promise is that my appreciation for you is real. I want to be someone who makes you feel respected, cared about, listened to, and valued. Someone who can make you laugh when you need it, listen when you need someone, and remind you that you never have to face a hard moment feeling completely alone.

There are people who come into your life and leave a little memory behind. Then there are people who become part of the memories you never want to forget. You are someone I genuinely want to keep making beautiful memories with — the silly ones, the quiet ones, the late-night conversations, the random jokes, and all the moments that don't seem important at the time but somehow become the ones you remember most.

So if you ever wonder why I made this for you, that's why. Because you matter to me. Because I wanted to put effort into something that would make you smile. And because sometimes a person deserves to be reminded that they are appreciated simply for being themselves.

I hope whenever you come back to this page, you remember that there is someone who genuinely cares about you, wants to see you happy, and appreciates every little thing that makes you, you. Whatever happens, I hope you always know that you are someone incredibly special, Jess. ❤️
